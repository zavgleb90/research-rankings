Welcome to the Global Research Rankings Project

This platform provides transparent, data-driven rankings of universities and authors based on publications across leading business and information systems journals. Our goal is to deliver a modern, user-friendly, and academically rigorous tool for exploring global research productivity.

Use the navigation menu above to explore:

  • Universities — Explore institutional research output across the full dataset.
  • Authors — Compare research productivity of individual scholars.
  • Journals — Analyze research output by journal.
  • Methodology — Learn how the rankings are computed.

This project is continuously evolving. Future features include university profiles, author profiles, fractional counting methods, collaboration networks, and interactive dashboards.
